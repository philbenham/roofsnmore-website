What we know about Seabrook roofs

Seabrook's storm-track roofing reality.

Seabrook catches hurricanes head-on, sits in salt-air country, and has the kind of complex bayside roof shapes that punish corner-cutting. We don't cut corners.

Direct hurricane exposure

Seabrook catches hurricane wind head-on. Six-nail shingle patterns, sealed ridge vent, storm-rated underlayment, and properly secured drip edge aren't extras here — they're the baseline. We don't cut corners on any of them.

Salt air and corrosion

Seabrook is on the water. Salt eats standard fasteners and flashing. We use stainless and aluminized fasteners, and we re-seal every penetration, because the bay is unforgiving.

Older bayside homes, complex roof shapes

Many Seabrook homes have multi-pitch designs, dormers, and significant valley work. Valleys are where amateur roofers cause leaks. We use peel-and-stick membrane in every valley, and we open up suspect ones to check for hidden rot before re-roofing.

Small town, big accountability

Seabrook isn't huge — word travels. We treat every job like our reputation depends on it, because in this town it does. Phil walks every inspection.

Common questions

Seabrook roofing questions.

Does Roofs-N-More work in Seabrook?

Yes — we serve all of Seabrook, from Todville Road to Lakewood Yacht Club to homes near Bayport. Kemah-based, 5 minutes away. Call 832-991-0928.

What's the best roof for a Seabrook home?

For most Seabrook homes near the bay, impact-resistant Class 4 architectural shingles plus enhanced wind-warranty installation give the best protection-to-cost mix. For homes directly on the water, we'll quote standing-seam metal alongside shingles so you can compare.

Are you available after hurricanes?

Yes. After named storms, Seabrook is one of our priority service areas. Free post-storm inspections, full photo documentation, and we'll attend the adjuster visit.
