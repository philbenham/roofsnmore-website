What we know about Houston roofs

Houston's roofing reality.

Greater Houston is huge and varied — different neighborhoods, different roof ages, different priorities. Here's what we see most often when we're inspecting homes in town.

Active hail belt

Greater Houston has been hit by multiple major hail events over the past decade. If your roof is on its second decade and hasn't been inspected since 2018, you may have damage that was never claimed — and every year without repair accelerates underlying deterioration.

Massive housing diversity

Houston covers everything from 1950s ranch homes inside the Loop to brand-new builds on the suburban edge. We work across all of it — and we'll tell you the truth about what your specific home actually needs, not what's easiest to sell.

Brutal summer UV

Houston summers cook asphalt shingles, especially on south-facing slopes. Quality underlayment, proper attic ventilation, and the right shingle weight class extend roof lifespan dramatically here. We dial all three in on every replacement.

Insurance claim navigation

Houston has more roofing companies than any city in Texas, and not all of them play it straight with carriers. We document damage honestly, work directly with adjusters, and never pressure you into work you don't need.

Houston roofing questions.

Does Roofs-N-More work in Houston?

Yes — we serve all corners of Greater Houston, from Westchase to Sugar Land to The Woodlands. Based in Kemah, we travel to wherever the work is. Call 832-991-0928 for a free estimate or inspection.

How much does roof replacement cost in Houston?

Houston roof replacements typically run $9,000–$24,000 depending on home size, pitch, and material. The wide range reflects how diverse Houston housing is — a small ranch home might be $9k while a 4,000-sq-ft home with steep pitches and architectural detailing pushes $24k. We give you a written, fixed-price estimate up front.

What's the best shingle for a Houston home?

For most Houston homeowners, impact-resistant Class 4 architectural shingles win. The hail history makes impact resistance worth it, and most carriers in Harris County offer 15–30% premium discounts that pay back the upgrade in 5–7 years. For homes with strong sun exposure, lighter-color blends also help cut summer cooling costs.
