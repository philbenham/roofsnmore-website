What we know about Pearland roofs

Pearland's roofing landscape.

Pearland's grown fast over the last 25 years, and the housing stock reflects it — original 1990s subdivisions, mid-2000s expansion, and brand-new 2020s builds all sit side by side.

Fast-growing, mixed-age housing

Pearland boomed through the 2000s and 2010s, so original 1990s-era roofs sit alongside roofs barely 5 years old. We tell you honestly which group your house is in — and what that means for your next move.

Brazoria County hail history

Pearland sits in an active hail corridor, with major events in 2018, 2021, and 2024 causing widespread damage that often went unclaimed. If your roof hasn't been inspected since 2021, it's worth a free look.

Clay soil and gutter strategy

Pearland's expanding clay soil is rough on foundations. Properly extended downspouts aren't optional here — they're foundation protection. Every gutter job we do in Pearland includes routing water well away from the slab.

Newer construction quirks

Many of Pearland's master-planned communities went up fast, and not every builder used premium underlayment or attic ventilation. By year 10–12, those shortcuts start to show. We can tell you whether yours is holding up or starting to slip.

Pearland roof replacements typically run $10,000–$22,000. Many Pearland homes are 2,500–3,500 sq ft with moderate pitches, which lands most jobs in the $13k–$18k range. Class 4 impact-resistant shingles add $1,500–$2,500 but usually pay back via insurance discounts.
