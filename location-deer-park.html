What we know about Deer Park roofs

Deer Park's roofing reality.

Deer Park's small enough that word travels — which is exactly the kind of community where a roofer's reputation actually means something.

Refinery-corridor exposure

Particulate-heavy air and high humidity are tough on shingles. Deer Park roofs sometimes look older than their actual age. Algae-resistant shingle blends and quality underlayment make a meaningful difference here.

Tight-knit community accountability

Deer Park is small enough that word travels. We treat every job like a future referral depends on it — because in this town, it does. Phil walks every inspection.

Mid-century to modern housing mix

Deer Park has 1950s–1970s homes alongside newer subdivisions. Older homes often have undersized attic ventilation by today's standards — we update it as part of every replacement.

Hurricane and severe storm zone

Deer Park catches its share of named-storm wind and hail. Six-nail shingle patterns, sealed ridge vents, and proper underlayment make the difference when conditions get serious.

Common questions

Deer Park roofing questions.

Does Roofs-N-More work in Deer Park?

Yes — we cover all of Deer Park from our Kemah base. Free inspections, fixed-price written estimates.

How much does roof replacement cost in Deer Park?

Deer Park roof replacements typically run $8,500–$18,000. Most homes are 1,500–2,500 sq ft on moderate roof pitches, which puts most jobs in the $11k–$14k range. We provide a written, fixed-price estimate up front — no surprises.

Do you handle storm-damage insurance claims?

Yes — we provide free inspections with photo documentation, and we can attend the adjuster visit. No pressure, no charge for the inspection regardless of whether you file a claim.
