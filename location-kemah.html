What we know about Kemah roofs

Kemah's bayside roofing reality.

Kemah sits right on Galveston Bay — and roofs here live a tougher life than roofs ten miles inland. Salt, wind, humidity, sun. We've been working on Kemah roofs for years and know what holds up.

Salt air corrosion

Kemah's right on the bay. Salt air eats unprotected metal — exposed nails, valley flashing, gutter joints, vent boots. We use stainless and aluminized fasteners, and we re-flash every penetration on every job, because the Gulf doesn't forgive shortcuts.

Direct hurricane wind exposure

Kemah catches hurricanes head-on. Properly nailed shingle patterns, six-nail vs. four-nail attachment, ridge vent securement — these aren't talking points here, they're real differences when 100+ mph gusts hit.

High humidity and attic ventilation

Kemah humidity wrecks underlayment if attic ventilation isn't dialed in. Ridge vent and soffit balance, proper baffles, and a current ventilation calculation are part of every Roofs-N-More job in town.

We're literally next door

If something needs attention, we're on the same side of the bridge as you. Punch lists get done. Warranty calls get answered. That matters more than any glossy marketing material.

Common questions

Kemah roofing questions.

Where in Kemah is Roofs-N-More based?

Kemah is our home base and we come to you — there is no storefront to visit. We've roofed homes in Lazy Bend, Anchor Bay, along Marina Bay Drive, and up Kipp Avenue. Call 832-991-0928 — Phil usually answers personally.

What roofing material works best in Kemah?

For most Kemah homes, impact-resistant Class 4 architectural shingles plus enhanced wind-warranty installation (six-nail pattern, sealed ridge) give the best mix of storm protection and cost. Standing-seam metal is also worth considering for homes near the waterfront — corrosion-protected metal handles salt air better than asphalt long-term.

Are you available after hurricanes?

Yes — fully insured (general liability and workers comp), with documentation available on request. After named storms, we prioritize Kemah and surrounding bay-area homes first. Free post-storm inspections, full photo documentation, and we'll attend the adjuster visit.
