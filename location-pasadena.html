What we know about Pasadena roofs

Pasadena's roofing reality.

Pasadena's housing stock spans 70+ years and sits in tougher-than-average air conditions. Honest, value-focused work is what most Pasadena homeowners are looking for — and that's what we do.

Industrial-corridor air conditions

Refinery emissions, particulate matter, and humidity wear shingles faster than typical Houston-area conditions. Pasadena roofs often look older than their actual age. Algae-resistant shingles and quality underlayment make a real difference here.

Working-family, value-focused

A lot of Pasadena homeowners aren't looking for fancy upgrades — they want a solid roof that holds up at a fair price. We respect that. Repairs when repairs make sense, replacements when they don't, and the work explained in plain English.

Active hail and storm zone

Pasadena gets its share of severe weather. Major hail events in recent years caused widespread damage, much of it never claimed. Free inspections, no obligation.

Homes built in waves

Pasadena's housing waves (1950s post-war, 1970s expansion, 1990s infill) all have their own roofing quirks — original decking thickness, attic ventilation styles, prior repair history. We've seen all of them and we know what to look for.

Common questions

Pasadena roofing questions.

Does Roofs-N-More work in Pasadena?

Yes — we cover all of Pasadena, including Deepwater, South Houston, and the Burke-Crenshaw area. Kemah-based, free estimates, no pressure.

How much does a roof replacement cost in Pasadena?

Pasadena roof replacements typically run $8,500–$18,000. Many homes are 1,400–2,200 sq ft, which puts most jobs in the $10k–$14k range. We give you a written, fixed-price estimate up front.

Do you do small repairs, not just full replacements?

Yes — we do leak repairs, partial re-roofs, valley fixes, ridge cap replacements, and storm-damage spot repairs. Not every roof needs a full replacement. We tell you the truth about what yours actually needs.
