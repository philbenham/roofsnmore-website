How much does a roof replacement cost in Alvin?

Alvin roof replacements typically run $9,000–$22,000 depending on home size. Larger Alvin homes (3,000+ sq ft) push toward the upper end. Class 4 impact-resistant shingles add $1,500–$2,500 but typically pay back via insurance premium savings.
